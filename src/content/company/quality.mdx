## Quality policy

We will not ship a piece we would not accept ourselves, and we will not describe a batch as
passing without a record that shows how it was checked. Where a buyer's standard is stricter
than ours, theirs applies.

## Testing

Production samples are tested for rub fastness, tensile strength at the seam, and hardware
pull resistance. Testing happens on material drawn from the actual production batch — a
prototype that passed six months ago says nothing useful about the hides currently on the floor.

## Inspection access

Buyers and their agencies are welcome at any stage of production, announced or unannounced.
Our inspection records, material certificates and process documentation are kept current and
available on site.
