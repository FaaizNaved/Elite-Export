Manufacturing leather at export volume is a problem of consistency, not capability. Any
workshop can produce one excellent headstall. Producing the four-hundredth to the same
standard, eleven weeks later, is the part buyers are actually paying for.

Our answer is to fix the route. Every order — fifty pieces or five thousand — moves through
the same eight stages in the same sequence, and each stage has a defined output that the next
stage will refuse if it is wrong.

## Why one craftsman carries a piece

Within assembly and finishing, a single craftsman takes a piece from cementing through to
final oiling. It is slower than a station line. It also means the tooling depth, the edge
burnish and the stitch tension stay consistent along the whole piece, because one pair of
hands set all three.

## Capacity

Sampling runs are handled away from the bulk lines, so prototype work never displaces
production already in progress. Capacity for a given order is confirmed against its
specification rather than quoted as a headline figure.
