## Production capacity

Three parallel lines give roughly 40,000 pieces a month across equestrian tack, bags and
accessories. Capacity is held in reserve for existing buyers during their peak season, which
is why our lead times hold when the market gets busy.

## Countries served

We currently ship to the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Australia,
Canada and the United Arab Emirates. Buyers in adjacent markets are welcome to enquire —
nothing about our process is market-specific.

## Minimum order quantities

Equestrian tack starts at 50 pieces per style. Bags and accessories start at 100 pieces.
Sampling orders are quoted separately and are not subject to the MOQ.
