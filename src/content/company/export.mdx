## Production capacity

Capacity is quoted against your actual order rather than advertised as a headline figure —
style, construction and finish change what a line can turn out in a week, so a single number
would be true of one product and misleading for the next. Send the specification and the
quantity and we will tell you what we can hold, and by when.

## Countries served

We currently ship to the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Australia,
Canada and the United Arab Emirates. Buyers in adjacent markets are welcome to enquire —
nothing about our process is market-specific.

## Minimum order quantities

Minimums depend on the construction, the hardware and whether the piece runs on our patterns
or yours. Tell us the style and we will confirm the minimum for it before you commit.
Sampling orders are quoted separately.
