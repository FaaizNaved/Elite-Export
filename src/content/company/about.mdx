Elite Export began in 1998 with four craftsmen, one clicking press and a single overseas
buyer. Today the same factory runs three production lines and ships to eight countries — but
the strap on a headstall is still cut, edged and burnished by a person, not a program.

## What we make

Western and English tack, full-grain leather bags, belts and accessories, produced under our
own patterns or to a buyer's specification. We are a manufacturer, not a trading house: every
piece that carries a customer's label is made in our own facility by our own staff.

## Vision

To be the manufacturer international buyers name first when they want leather goods that
survive their warranty period — and the one their customers name when asked where the piece
came from.

## Mission

Build leather goods that outlast the season they were bought in, using materials we can trace
and processes we can evidence, at a price that lets our buyers compete.

## How we work

Every order moves through the same route: hide selection, pattern approval, cutting,
assembly, finishing, inspection, packing. Nothing skips a stage because a shipment is late —
the inspection report goes out before the container does.

## Infrastructure

The facility runs three production lines across 45,000 square feet, with a dedicated pattern
studio, a finishing hall and a climate-controlled leather store that holds roughly six weeks
of production stock. Keeping material on site is what lets us quote realistic lead times
rather than optimistic ones.
