This policy explains how Elite Export Pvt. Ltd. handles information collected through this
website.

## What we collect

We collect only what you send us: the details you enter into the contact and buyer enquiry
forms — name, company, country, email, phone and your message — together with standard
server logs and privacy-respecting analytics.

## Why we collect it

To answer your enquiry, to prepare quotations, and to keep a record of business
correspondence. We do not sell or rent your details, and we do not use them for automated
decision-making.

## How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is retained for three years. Server logs are retained for 30 days.

## Your rights

Write to us at the address on the contact page to request a copy of the information we hold
about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted.
