The clicking press is where consistency is won or lost. A die cut under even pressure gives an
edge that needs minimal sanding; an uneven cut leaves a crushed edge that will never burnish
cleanly no matter how much time the finishing bench spends on it.

Stroke depth is set micrometrically for each die and each leather substance, then proved on a
test cut before the batch runs.
