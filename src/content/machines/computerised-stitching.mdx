Machine stitching is not a compromise — on a two-metre rein, a programmed head holds stitch
length far more consistently than any hand can. Where it does not belong is at stress points,
which we saddle-stitch by hand because a lock stitch unravels from a single broken thread and a
saddle stitch does not.
