The Heritage messenger is our highest-volume bag pattern and the one we recommend to buyers
entering the leather goods category for the first time. It uses the same hides and the same
saddle-stitching as our tack line, so it ages the way customers expect leather to age.

## Construction

The flap and body are cut from a single hide wherever the order size allows, so grain and
tone stay consistent across the visible panels. Stress points — strap anchors, gusset
corners, flap hinge — are saddle-stitched by hand with bonded nylon rather than rivetted.

## Private label

Debossed or foil-stamped logos on the flap, custom hardware finishes, and bespoke lining
fabrics are all available at standard MOQ.
