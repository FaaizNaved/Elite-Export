The browband pattern spreads pressure across the forehead rather than concentrating it at
the ear, which suits horses worked in a bit for long sessions and buyers selling into
schooling and lesson markets.

## Fit

Both cheeks adjust independently, so the bit can be levelled without unbuckling the crown.
The throatlatch is removable — buyers who sell into show markets usually order it fitted;
trail and ranch buyers often leave it off.

## Buckstitch

Contrast latigo is threaded by hand through punched slots. It is slower than machine
stitching and it is the detail buyers photograph, so we do not shortcut it.
