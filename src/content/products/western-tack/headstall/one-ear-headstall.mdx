The one ear headstall is the pattern most of our western buyers start with, and the one
they keep reordering. It carries no browband and no throatlatch — the ear slot alone holds
the crown in place, which keeps weight off the poll and the horse's field of view clear.

## How it is made

Each headstall begins as a full side of vegetable-tanned harness leather. Straps are cut
along the backbone, where the fibre structure is tightest and stretch is most predictable,
then split to an even 9–10 oz before tooling begins.

The floral border is cut and bevelled by hand. One craftsman carries a headstall from
casing through to final oiling, so the tooling depth stays consistent along the whole strap
rather than varying between stations.

## Finishing

Edges are bevelled, sanded, and burnished in three passes with a waxed slicker. The result
is a sealed edge that resists sweat and water rather than one that simply looks smooth on
the day it ships.

## Customisation

We produce this pattern to buyer specification: alternative tooling patterns, buckstitch in
your own colourway, stainless or nickel hardware, and branded conchos. Send a reference
sample or drawing and we will return a prototype for approval before bulk production.
